Investment
The Investor will provide $100.00 USD for the first live trading attempt of the
bot or model operated by the Operator.

Profit Split
If the first attempt is profitable, net profits will be split 50% to the
Investor and 50% to the Operator.

Net Profits
Net profits means realized gains minus trading losses, exchange fees,
transaction costs, and other direct agreed expenses.

Loss and Retry Terms
If the initial $100.00 USD is lost, the Operator may request up to two
additional attempts, but only after an agreed improvement to the model. Each
additional attempt requires the Investor's approval and separate funding.

Source Code Access
If the bot is profitable on the first attempt, the Investor will be given access
to the source code. Access to the source code does not transfer ownership of the
intellectual property unless both parties agree otherwise in writing.

Risk
The Investor understands that trading is speculative and that the full
investment may be lost. The Operator makes no guarantee of profit or capital
preservation.

This document is a summary of business terms for discussion purposes and is
non-binding unless replaced by a signed definitive agreement.
